# The Metamorphosis of Legible Studio

**Date:** September 8, 2026  
**Event:** The pivot from software company to design methodology  
**What survives:** LegiCAD (Legi)

---

## What Legible Studio Was

Legible Studio began as a software company with a specific thesis:

> **Replace paper plans in residential construction with AR hardware (LegiView) + structured plan software (Legible Studio).**

It had:
- A pitch deck (May 2026)
- A target investor (Baukunst)
- An ask ($1.59M USD)
- A roadmap (v0 tethered prototype → v0.5 custom optics → v1 safety glasses)
- A Rust codebase that generated permit-ready drawing sets
- A Vulkan viewer in development

It was, in the user's own words, "in metamorphosis — becoming something new."

## What Happened

**The spark:** Baukunst supplied the spark. The user took it and ran in a different direction.

**The realization:** Working on the voice assistant and SemOS revealed the real problem with design software — they have to be specific to be effective. Ontario is the right market. But the software-as-a-company model was the wrong container.

**The pivot:**
- Baukunst pitch: **abandoned**
- Legible Studio as software company: **dissolved**
- Legible Studio as a **design methodology**: **emerges**
- The product that survives: **LegiCAD**

## What Legible Studio Becomes

Legible Studio is no longer a software company. It is:

> **A design practice that uses regime architecture, question-based design, and parametric constraint to produce compliant housing — starting in Sudbury, Ontario.**

The software (LegiCAD) is a **tool** the practice uses, not the company itself.

**The practice:**
- Jer designs buildings using LegiCAD
- Students contribute to projects and the tool
- Real projects in Sudbury over 3-5 years
- The loop proves itself through built work

**The tool (LegiCAD):**
- Computer Assisted Design, as it should be
- Parametric massing from regulated space
- Human choice from statistical constraint
- Open source, on GitHub

## The Philosophy

The shift mirrors the Regime Architecture methodology itself:

> Data does not replace judgment. Data removes the impossible. The architect chooses among what remains.

Legible Studio as a company was an impossibility — the funding path, the hardware timeline, the market timing. The practice that emerged from dissolving that constraint is what remains.

**The user is the center of gravity.** All projects orbit them. The business model serves the person, not the other way around.
